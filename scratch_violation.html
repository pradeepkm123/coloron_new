    What we offer

    Hikvision's Violation Detection System uses multi-frame recognition and vehicle tracking technology to gather driving information. It judges a driver’s direction and route, and detects vehicle traffic violations. It can effectively regulate driver behavior, reducing traffic accidents, ensuring road safety, and improving the efficiency of road safety management for law enforcement.

    Yellow box junction violation detection

    Crossing the gore area detection

    Illegal parking detection

    Key technology

    Traffic light enhancement technology 

     

    • Render realistic, natural imaging

    • Remain unaffected by traffic light shaking

    • Are not affected by changes from day to night

    • Resolves issues around green lights appearing white

    • Crossing the gore area detection
    • Illegal parking detection

    Guaranteed safety of drivers & pedestrians in orderly traffic

    • Detects a variety of traffic violations including red light running, wrong-way driving, not following the directional sign, illegal lane change, weaving out of the lane, speeding, etc.
    • Provides abundant vehicle feature data – license plate number, vehicle color, manufacturer, type
